SWARIFLY® MANIFESTO — EST. 2018

ARCHITECTURE FOR THOUGHT

Founded in 2018, SWARIFLY® treats everyday stationery with the rigor of Swiss industrial architecture. Every notebook is Smyth-sewn to lie completely flat at 180 degrees, milled from acid-free 120gsm–300gsm cotton rag that resists fountain pen feathering.

Our writing instruments are turned from solid aerospace aluminum, raw C360 brass, and matte-anodized titanium—balanced to the millimeter for fatigue-free drafting and long-form writing.
